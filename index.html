RF Concepts

1. Radio Frequency

Radio frequency (RF) refers to electromagnetic signals used for wireless communication.

2. Antenna Gain

Antenna gain describes how effectively an antenna directs energy compared with a reference antenna. It is commonly expressed in dBi.

3. Wavelength

Wavelength is the distance travelled by a wave during one cycle.

Formula: λ = c / f

4. Free-Space Path Loss

Signal power decreases as a radio wave travels through free space.

FSPL (dB) = 32.44 + 20 log₁₀(f MHz) + 20 log₁₀(d km)
