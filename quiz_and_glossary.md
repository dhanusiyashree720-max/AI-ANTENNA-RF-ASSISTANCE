Part A: RF Glossary

1. Antenna

A device that transmits or receives electromagnetic waves.

2. Dipole Antenna

An antenna consisting of two conductive elements, commonly designed to operate around a specified wavelength.

3. Wavelength (λ)

The distance between equivalent points on consecutive cycles of a wave. In free space, wavelength is calculated using λ = c/f.

4. Frequency (f)

The number of wave cycles per second, measured in hertz (Hz).

5. Antenna Gain

A measure of an antenna's directional radiation performance relative to a reference antenna.

6. dBi

A logarithmic unit expressing antenna gain relative to an ideal isotropic antenna.

7. Impedance

The opposition a circuit presents to alternating current, expressed in ohms (Ω).

8. Impedance Matching

The process of choosing or transforming impedances to achieve a desired power-transfer or reflection condition.

9. Reflection Coefficient (Γ)

A complex quantity representing the ratio of reflected voltage wave to incident voltage wave at a specified reference plane.

10. VSWR

Voltage Standing Wave Ratio, a measure of the standing-wave condition on a transmission line.

11. Characteristic Impedance (Z₀)

The voltage-to-current ratio of a single travelling wave on a transmission line.

12. Link Budget

An accounting of transmitter power, antenna gains, propagation losses, and other gains and losses to estimate received power.

13. Free-Space Path Loss (FSPL)

The ideal propagation loss between antennas in free space under the assumptions of the free-space model.

14. dB

A logarithmic unit used to express a ratio of power or other quantities.

15. dBm

An absolute power level expressed in decibels relative to 1 milliwatt.

16. Radiation Pattern

A representation of how an antenna's radiation varies with direction.

17. Transmitter

Equipment that generates and sends a signal.

18. Receiver

Equipment that detects and processes a received signal.

19. Frequency Band

A specified range of operating frequencies.

20. Return Loss

A logarithmic measure of reflected power relative to incident power, commonly expressed as a positive value for a passive load with less than total reflection.
